# CODEFUSION 2k26: Build the Future, Shape Tomorrow
![CodeFusion Banner](images/codefusion_banner.jpeg)

**India's Premier National-Level Hackathon**

CodeFusion 2K26 is a flagship national-level hackathon supported by Microsoft, bringing together the brightest innovators, developers, designers, and problem-solvers from across India. Designed to inspire groundbreaking innovation, the event provides a platform where ambitious ideas transform into impactful solutions that address real-world challenges.

## Event Details
- **Type:** Offline
- **Venue:** Echelon Institute Of Technology, Faridabad, Haryana, India
- **Team Size:** 2 - 5 Members
- **Prize Pool:** ₹2,00,000 to be won + Exciting rewards + Certificates
- **Tracks:** Artificial Intelligence, Software Development, Web3, Blockchain, and Open Innovation

## Eligibility
- Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) students across India (Engineering Students)
- Students from all branches and disciplines are welcome
- Cross-college and interdisciplinary teams are highly encouraged
- Passion for innovation and problem-solving is all you need
- **Note:** A valid college ID card is mandatory for participation.

## Stages and Timelines

### Round 1: Online PPT Submission Round
- **Timeline:** 24 Apr 26, 07:32 PM IST --> 10 Aug 26, 11:59 AM IST (Registration & PPT submission deadline: 10 Aug 26, 11:59 PM IST)
- Teams will develop and submit their solution in the form of a PPT based on the hackathon themes. The submission should clearly present the idea, approach, key features, technical implementation, and expected impact.
- All entries will be evaluated by the screening panel, and shortlisted teams will advance to the next stage of the hackathon, leading to the final round.

### Round 2: Grand Finale (Offline Round)
- **Timeline:** 21 Aug 26, 08:23 AM IST --> 22 Aug 26, 07:24 PM IST
- Shortlisted teams from the online round will advance to the on-ground grand finale at Echelon Institute of Technology, Faridabad. During the 36-hour offline round, teams will further develop and refine their solutions and present their final projects before a panel of judges. Evaluation will be based on innovation, technical implementation, feasibility, impact, and overall presentation.

## Winning Ideas & Winners

🏆 **1st Prize:** **Team Manas** 
**Project:** VakyaSetu
**Description:** VakyaSetu is an IoT-based assistive communication system designed for paralysis patients who may have difficulty speaking or using conventional input devices. It uses a custom built capacitive touch sensor interface to detect intentional touch inputs and translate them into predefined words or messages, enabling patients to communicate more independently.

🥈 **2nd Prize:** **Neogen Innovators**
**Project:** NextTrack
**Description:** NextTrack uses Long-range Modulation to transmit messages (mostly string data) over long distances without using any internet. It consists of three main components:
1. Main control panel
2. Nodes for network establishment
3. Watch for the user when they are not near a pole

🥉 **3rd Prize:** **Team X**
**Project:** AEGIS (Autonomous Emergency Geospatial Intelligence System)
**Description:** AEGIS is an AI-powered disaster response platform designed for private NGOs, humanitarian organizations, weather forecasters, and independent relief coordinators who operate outside government systems. It provides enterprise-grade situational awareness through a rotating 3D globe that tracks 26+ live disasters from NASA, USGS, and ReliefWeb, while running physics-based simulations for five hazard types (flood, earthquake, wildfire, cyclone, chemical release) with 120-minute predictive timelines. Organizations can model evacuation scenarios for their specific facilities—refugee camps, hospitals, distribution centers—using high-fidelity digital twins that calculate optimized routes considering real-time hazard spread and infrastructure damage. Built entirely on zero-cost infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers, MapLibre GL, OpenStreetMap), AEGIS delivers sub-20ms response times globally without subscriptions or API keys, democratizing sophisticated disaster intelligence for NGOs and community responders who are often first on the ground but lack access to expensive government-grade tools.
